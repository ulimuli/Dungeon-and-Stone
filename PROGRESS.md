## Implemented

- Godot4.5.1 offline desktop game at640×360 with integer scaling; keyboard/mouse, remapping, controller input paths, text scale, reduced motion, audio/mute and persistent settings. Multiple atomic save slots with checksum, backup recovery and structural validation; schema3 minute clock migrates schema2 safely.
- Six playable races: Barbarian, Beastman, Draconian, Dwarf, Fairy, Human. Creation/companion customization uses modular32px directional sprites and64px portraits, saved independently of combat RNG. Twelve generated recruits, two templates per race, with distinct saved names/looks. Novel companion identities are not used. Companion figures remain omitted from central Rafdonia.
- Rafdonia spatial city map,12 service operators, shopping/selling, equipment, storage, repair/rest, recruitment, essence tubes, magic tuition and hardship work. Six racial lands,36 interactive points,18 local NPC roles, one-time Barbarian coming-of-age weapon exchange and three permanent Draconian blessing choices. Location/access/fees/initial Fairy lesson remain provisional where unspecified.
- Shared102-stat catalog and modifier pipeline for items, essences, race, statuses, derived combat/resource/awareness values and informational indices. Every registered entry has a consumer or explained summary calculation. This is not every stat in the novel. See STAT_REFERENCE.md.
- Racial entry mechanics: Undying I, Beastman destructive Skadia awakening, Dwarf Braces duration, controllable Draconian Fear, Fairy Fire spirit/light and Spirit Stone10% additional Wind attempt, Human aptitude assessment/private Ice Spear lessons. Full racial/profession trees remain unfinished.
- **Nine dungeon areas across floors1–3:** Crystal Cave; Goblin Forest, Land of the Dead, Black Rock Mountain, Den of Beasts; Ironrock Hill, Orc Colony, Witch's Forest, Dark River Estuary.96×64 reproducible generated layouts, minimum three-wide travel paths (forest five), loops, fog, persistent discoveries and per-area expedition memory.
- Crystal Cave has four separate directional steles, a distinct dark central Great Sage memorial, lit ordinary arrivals, dark outskirts, sector-specific native species and northern goblin traps. Den has cliff caves, deeper black canyons and hidden sewage pits. Forest goblins form three estimated15-member groups. Dead has ruins, vines and Day2 corpse-flower water harvesting. Magic Torches burn about three days and change dark-zone visibility; shared water and food create real recovery/expedition choices. Grid distances, quantities and rates are estimates.
- Source-backed Den→Ironrock and Dead→Estuary entry routes. Compact paths connect Hill→Orcs→Witch and Estuary→Witch, representing documented journeys with estimated adjacency/scale. Return anchors and one absolute expedition clock persist across travel. Floors close after7/10/15days; closed lower floors cannot be reentered. Survivors automatically return to Rafdonia. GoblinForest and BlackRock third-floor entry destinations remain unavailable pending their own playable habitats.
- Four third-floor terrain identities: hill terraces/passes, Orc woodland clearings, looped Witch forest, shallow estuary channels/dry red mounds. Witch field nullifies non-field statuses, applies disorientation/hallucinations/pain amplification through shared stats, and attracts an actual Carnivaro after stationary exposure. Endpoint intervals follow documented1h→10min at84h; intermediate interpolation is not invented. Purple-tree cabin refuge disables field/hostile actions, has a maximum five living occupants and expires from appearance rather than entry; late camp recovery scales to remaining refuge time. Fixed appearance cadence is an estimate. Estuary high/rising tide constrains camping to dry mounds; no invented drowning damage.
- **35 obtainable encounter species** including the Witch Eye hunter,29 item definitions,7 partial essence kits,12 generated recruits,1 spell,3 Numbered Items. Fourteen new third-floor species have original32px two-pose silhouettes. Unknown complete skill/attribute packages remain explicitly incomplete. Carnivaro Mana Mine/Hound and Frogman Shaman's full spells are not claimed.
