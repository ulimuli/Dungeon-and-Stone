# Dungeon & Stone

An unfinished offline desktop reconstruction of the game described in *Surviving the Game as a Barbarian*. Godot 4.5.1, typed GDScript, 640×360 with integer scaling.

## Play

Download `Dungeon-and-Stone-macOS.dmg` from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/ulimuli/Dungeon-and-Stone/releases), then open **Dungeon & Stone.app**. The universal macOS runtime supports Apple Silicon and Intel; no editor, account or network is needed. The ZIP is an alternative. This local build is ad-hoc signed, not notarized.

New Game → choose one of six races → customize → prepare on the Rafdonia map → enter through Dimensional Square. Click buildings or people for services. Tooltips contain details; the journal records discoveries.

WASD/arrows move; click an enemy to attack; E interacts; Space ends the party phase; Tab selects an ally; 1–3 select techniques, then click a target. I inventory, P status, J journal, C eight-hour rest, Escape menu. Keyboard bindings and audio/text/motion settings are configurable.

Crystal Cave closes at 168 expedition hours; second-floor areas close at 240. Survivors return automatically. An arrival portal is not an immediate exit. Loose essences expire after about thirty minutes; confirm absorption while adjacent. Death is permanent.

## Scope

Three areas, seven species, three implemented essence families, fourteen item definitions and six named companion profiles are integrated. Dwalkie can use Ice Spear; the companions' full novel kits are unfinished. The six race profiles are selectable and visually distinct; their full racial progression is unfinished. There is no complete campaign, final boss or ending yet. Prices, starting stats, damage coefficients, tactical timing and recruitment placement remain explicitly unverified.
