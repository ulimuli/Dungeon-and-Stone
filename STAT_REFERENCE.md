# Shared stat reference

Generated from `game/stat_catalog.gd`. 102 registered IDs: 102 with simulation consumers or informational summaries, 0 catalogued for future implementation. This is the known catalog in this project, not a claim that all of the novel's thousands of substats are reconstructed.

## Numeric modifier format

Equipment, essences and racial definitions use `modifiers` entries with `stat`, `op` and `value`. The owning definition supplies stable provenance automatically. Saved unit modifiers additionally provide unique `id` and `source` fields. `base_stats` supplies explicit intrinsic baselines. Registry defaults are calculator fallbacks, not novel starting attributes.

```json
{"modifiers":[{"stat":"attack","op":"add","value":8},{"stat":"attack","op":"multiply","value":1.25},{"stat":"armor","op":"add","value":3}]}
```

Operations: `add` is a flat bonus; `increase` is an additive fractional increase (0.25 means25%); `multiply` is an independent factor (1.25 means×1.25); `at_least` sets a lower result bound. Formula: `(base + flat bonuses) × (1 + summed increases) × multiplied factors`, then minimum bound, stat rounding and caps. Source IDs sort deterministically; duplicate modifier IDs, unknown stats, NaN/Infinity and invalid combined results are rejected. Primary attributes feed derived values before secondary modifiers. Muscle Mass contributes its sourced1% total Strength per point.

Raw resistance points, flat armor, direct percentage reduction and damage multipliers are distinct. The current raw-point conversion `100×points/(100+abs(points))`, tactical bounds, HP/attack/resource formulas and most baseline values are explicit estimates. Known novel coefficients below weight aggregate indices; they are not damage-reduction percentages. Stateful abilities such as storing prevented damage, suppression, summoning or essence sealing still use the shared effect rules.
