# Early-floor source audit

Read 2026-11-05. Original-novel passages take priority; the accessible mirrors below are translations, sometimes machine translated. Names vary (Den of Beasts / Beast’s Lair, Wall-Mole Rat / Wall Mole, Vulkars / Bulkar). A translation is evidence of a described mechanic, not exact engine formulas or coordinates. No source reviewed supplies a complete map, encounter schedule, damage spreadsheet or exhaustive floor population. A perfect reconstruction cannot be claimed from this audit.

## Crystal Cave: lighting and entrances

[Novel4](https://hennoveltranslations.com/episodes/stgb-episode-4/) establishes randomized arrival, normally near light-emitting wall crystals. Darkness is mainly in the outer routes toward second-floor portals; the narrator's initial dark spawn is abnormal. Goblin traps have nearby goblins on this floor. [Novel5](https://hennoveltranslations.com/episodes/stgb-episode-5/) shows a hand touching an outer dead-end stele opening a second-floor portal. It distinguishes the narrator's exceptionally remote starting position from normal arrivals. It does not say the central commemorative monument selects ordinary second-floor destinations.

## Compass routes and the central monument

| Direction | Destination | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| North | Goblin Forest | [Novel11–12](https://martialrealm.com/novel/sgb/chapter-10-11-12/): repeated northward travel, portal discovery, then Forest arrival. |
| East | Den of Beasts | [Novel50](https://koreanplayer.com/novel/surviving-the-game-as-a-barbarian-novel/chapter-49-50-51/): explicit eastern first-floor portal. |
| South | Black Rock Mountain | [Novel27](https://koreanplayer.com/novel/surviving-the-game-as-a-barbarian-novel/chapter-25-26-27/): explicit southern route. |
| West | Land of the Dead | Inference from the four-region topology, western ghouls and the western-edge return journey in Novel27. The other three routes are directly anchored above. |

Novel27 describes the first floor's directional habitats: eastern blade wolves, western ghouls, southern gnomes and northern goblins. Its central dark clearing mixes all four, with relatively sparse monsters, a roughly30m radius and a monument to the Great Sage. This is separate from the ordinary boundary portal steles. The monument can force a rift using species-specific stones and a rank-eight sacrifice. The narrator brings stones from the four first-floor species plus Death Fiend; offering Death Fiend selects the Bloody Citadel/Crimson Fortress. Rift cooldown is at least three city cycles; random appearance is typically later. Exact complete ritual sequence, first-opener competition and other rift variants need further implementation research. The monument should not silently become a free ordinary floor selector.

## Magic Torch

[Novel23](https://martialrealm.com/novel/sgb/chapter-22-23-24/) describes Magic Torches lasting about three days, priced10,000 stones. Grid visibility radius, compressed price and exact saved-minute conversion remain implementation choices.

## Goblin Forest

[Novel11–12](https://martialrealm.com/novel/sgb/chapter-10-11-12/) describes a forest under a luminous high cavern ceiling, permanently dim rather than a normal day/night sky. The arrival clearing has roughly50m radius with no creatures visible. Groups are approximately15, compared with first-floor groups of3–4. Swordsman/archer variants and stronger monsters become more frequent outward. Forest traps occur independently of nearby goblins and can be triggered with thrown stones; they obstruct retreat. First-floor return is through the arrival portal before the lower floor closes. Tree chopping with the narrator's early hammer is ineffective; do not award ordinary wood harvesting without a suitable supported rule.

## Land of the Dead

[Novel23–24](https://koreanplayer.com/novel/surviving-the-game-as-a-barbarian-novel/chapter-22-23-24/) describes dark black wet ground, stone ruins and no ordinary traps. Elder Ghoul, Skeleton, Skeleton Warrior and Skeleton Archer kills each show+1XP. Elder Ghouls are twice ordinary Ghoul size. Banshees rarely initiate; attacking first causes Mark of Resentment until expedition exit. Pure physical attackers cannot handle them in this scene. Day1 flowers are not available until the following day; blossoms on ruin-covering vines contain drinkable water when split. Exact yield/regrowth is unknown. Death Fiend's essence offers Call of the Dead (Ghoul summons) and Physical Preservation (regeneration), with weaker recovery than the monster itself. Damage, durations, aggro radius and universal immunity formulas are not given.

The [second-floor wiki](https://surviving-the-game-as-a-barbarian.fandom.com/wiki/Labyrinth/Second_Floor) lists Banshee, Death Fiend and Dullahan as well as the above undead; Dullahan is described as an intermediate boss-like enemy. This does not define a complete Dullahan kit.

## Den of Beasts

[Novel50](https://koreanplayer.com/novel/surviving-the-game-as-a-barbarian-novel/chapter-49-50-51/) describes a stinking canyon maze, towering cliffs, monster caves and hidden sewage-filled pits rather than Goblin traps. Explicit kills: Giant Blade Wolf+1, Sabertooth Tiger+2, Werewolf+2, Wall Mole+1; later Half-Moon Rock Bear+1, Wall Mole Queen+1 and Bloodstained Blade Wolf+1. Bulkar is also named. Cliffs change red-brown to black in the later portion, where rank-nine monsters stop and rank-eight groups increase. Exact species-specific attack/essence kits are not established by this chapter. Monster names do not justify invented special skills presented as canon.

## Black Rock Mountain

[Novel27](https://koreanplayer.com/novel/surviving-the-game-as-a-barbarian-novel/chapter-25-26-27/) explicitly identifies the southern route's gnomes, kobolds and Stone Golems. Stone Golem Suppression needs preparation; related earlier scenes establish any positive damage can free a suppressed victim. [Second-floor wiki](https://surviving-the-game-as-a-barbarian.fandom.com/wiki/Labyrinth/Second_Floor) additionally lists Kobold Shield Soldier, Sandman, Sandworm and Death Worm. Later-chapter worm behavior/ranks need separate research; do not grant every listed monster an assumed essence.

## Integration priorities and remaining limits

1. Correct all four compass routes and directional first-floor populations; keep central monument distinct from ordinary portal steles.
2. Give the missing Den canyon/cave/pit geography rather than copying a forest layout.
3. Use real Forest groups/variants/trap density and Dead ruins/corpse-flower landmarks. Calibrate grid density and resource timing as explicit estimates.
4. Add only verified species kits; unsupported exact coefficients remain marked provisional. Existing broad generic tactics must not be described as full novel kits.
5. Preserve visibility, connectivity, reachable portals, party placement and lower-floor closure. Novel scale/travel times are not equivalent to96×64 one-minute tiles.
6. Rifts, floor masters, complete elite/Guardian variants, full water needs, roaming explorer factions and actual exhaustive early-floor content remain separate unfinished work.
