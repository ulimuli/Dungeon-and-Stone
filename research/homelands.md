# Six homelands: source and adaptation notes

Research date: 2026-11-05. The catalog separates readable in-world dialogue from source/estimate metadata. All service operators are authored local roles; no novel companion has been silently assigned a shop or recruitment post. The six compact maps, point placement and walking distances are adaptations, not reproduced novel geography.

## Important practices represented

- **Barbarian coming of age:** the weapon clearing offers one permanent choice from six existing weapons before the hero's first expedition. It exchanges the worn starter club directly; item quality/statistics and eligibility window are provisional, with no repeat gift/refund. Training is an observed drill rather than an XP/stat farm.
- **Barbarian tattoos, hair and funerals:** the [Barbarians wiki](https://surviving-the-game-as-a-barbarian.fandom.com/wiki/Barbarians) describes childhood tattoo circuits and funeral offerings of cut hair or blood. [Novel782](https://hennoveltranslations.org/episodes/surviving-the-game-as-a-barbarian-episode-782/) identifies the traditional exposure of bodies to wind and rain. Here these customs are optional lore/remembrance; no injury, blood collection or cosmetic change is forced.
- **Beastman Soul Beast rite:** the guide leads into the already implemented Skadia confirmation. The ring landmark explains sealing and the risk to worn equipment. Bag preservation and the actual contract remain runtime rules.
- **Dwarf Blessing of Arms:** [Novel52](https://hennoveltranslations.com/episodes/stgb-episode-52/) and the [Dwarves wiki](https://surviving-the-game-as-a-barbarian.fandom.com/wiki/Dwarves) connect the innate blessing to Numbered Item efficiency/cooldowns. The current supported Guardian’s Braces duration benefit is a specific implementation. Artifact inspection is read-only; no invented paid blessing initiation is sold. The smith uses the ordinary repair command.
- **Fairy spirit development:** [chapters19–21](https://martialrealm.com/novel/sgb/chapter-19-20-21/) support Spirit Stones for additional affinities, ten-percent success, and Wind guiding arrows. First Fire Spirit lesson acquisition/fee, current Wind-only availability and numerical combat conversion remain explicit provisional mechanics. The three teaching points delegate to their actual existing commands.
- **Human profession instruction:** assessment and tuition remain separate commitments, using the existing fees, time, aptitude and essence restrictions. The notice board explains specialization without selling unfinished occupations.
- **Draconian restraint:** the mentor offers the innate Fear toggle. The shaman offers the three blessings from chapter202; Dragon Language remains unfinished. The temple does not grant free teleportation.

## Shared content protocol

`HomelandCatalog.definitions()` returns six ancestry-keyed entries with `name`, `theme`, `description`, `reference_urls`, `estimate_notes`, `provisional_location`, `npcs`, `landmarks`, and `points`. Each land has three NPCs and three landmarks. `points` is NPCs followed by landmarks; `points(ancestry)` and `point(ancestry,id)` return safe copies for runtime decoration.

Point rows contain stable `id`, `name`, `role`, `ancestry`, `line`, `message`, `action`, `kind`, `reference_urls`, `authored=true`, and `canonical_identity=false`. Geometry belongs to the map provider, so catalog rows have no `pos`. Semantic actions are `coming_of_age`, `dragon_blessing`, `undying`, `skadia`, `artifact_blessing`, `spirit_lesson`, `spirit_stone`, `wind_guidance`, `magic_assess`, `magic_study`, `dragon_fear`, `lore`, `rest`, `training`, `repair`, and `return`. Rules, simulation and UI own eligibility, effects, confirmation, payment, elapsed time and persistence. Notes do not turn unavailable mechanics into active services.

## Uncertainties to retain

English names vary between Rafdonia/Raphdonia and several character/place transliterations. Gnome Tree is shared economic terrain and is also on the route to the Dragon sanctuary; forge activity alone does not prove it is an exclusive Dwarf homeland. The Dwarf staging-area choice must therefore retain its explicit provisional source note. The Beastman council description currently relies on a secondary compilation, while Fairy Barharium has a direct novel citation. No sanctuary map here reproduces exact canonical coordinates.

## Dragon blessings

[Chapter202](https://hennoveltranslations.com/archives/episodes/stgb-episode-202) establishes Earth essence attributes+20%, Volcano skill performance about+30%, and Sea Soul Power+100. The native-Draconian level2/500-stone single-slot rite is provisional playable access, not a source claim of a public blessing market. The foreigner exception's Dragon Slayer bargain is unavailable. Earth scales signed flat essence attributes only. Volcano maps performance onto current essence status/field durations, scent radius and learned-spell damage; whole-round/tile rounding is estimated and costs/range/cooldowns/artifact/racial effects remain separate. Sea supplies the raw Soul Power stat without free refill.
