# Combat stat audit

Qualitative evidence and numerical reconstruction must stay separate. No files outside the delegated helper/tests and this requested note were changed.

| Stat/category | Verified evidence | Implementation limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cold Sensitivity | [Novel 279](https://hennoveltranslations.com/archives/episodes/stgb-episode-279), lines413–417: enhancing Frost Condensation raises frost sensitivity and frost damage scales with that value. [Novel103 text](https://novelnuku.com/surviving-the-game-as-a-barbarian-en-chapter-103/) explicitly explains corresponding elemental power follows sensitivity. | Matching outgoing elemental power only; it is not incoming cold vulnerability. A1%/point curve bounded0.1..5 is an estimate. |
| Cutting Force | [Novel279](https://hennoveltranslations.com/archives/episodes/stgb-episode-279), lines459–466: strengthened cutting power improves the sword's cut, which still stops at hard bone. [Novel training-scene text](https://www.webnovel.com/es/book/35042645608449005/94222059189632582) demonstrates improved sword cutting against steel after conditional effects become Cutting Force. | Apply only explicitly slashing physical weapon hits. No universal armor bypass, blunt damage or elemental proc scaling is established. Same1%/point factor is estimated. |
| Critical | [Novel841](https://hennoveltranslations.com/archives/episodes/surviving-the-game-as-a-barbarian-episode-841/) says a skill guarantees a critical next strike and the damage multiplier varies by monster weak point. [Novel279](https://hennoveltranslations.com/archives/episodes/stgb-episode-279), lines148–152, names critical among combat stats raised by weapon proficiency. | Caller supplies the factor;1.5 default and numeric chance conversion are estimates. Helper conservatively permits normal weapon hits only; no fixed/DOT/secondary/spell crit extension was verified. Zero chance leaves RNG unchanged. |
| Pain Resistance | [Novel35 text](https://bape-clothing.com/book/surviving-the-game-as-a-barbarian-2/chapter-35) connects absorption of+70 Pain Tolerance with immediate pain relief while bone protection is separately attributed to Bone Density. [Novel56 text](https://novelnuku.com/surviving-the-game-as-a-barbarian-en-chapter-56/) shows tolerance to the forest's amplified pain. [Novel851 text](https://www.webnovel.com/book/surviving-the-game-as-barbarian_36909131708472705/surviving-the-game-as-a-barbarian-chapter-851_99335260168316696) describes lowered Pain Resistance making sensation and burning pain more intense. | Sensory intensity only. No evidence for HP reduction, AP refund, injury prevention, stun immunity or generic status resistance. Estimated100 points halves perceived pain;−100 doubles it, bounded0.1..5. |
| Lightning Resistance | [Novel108 text](https://noveltune.net/book/surviving-the-game-as-a-barbarian-2/108.html) identifies Tarunbas's negative Lightning Resistance and lightning vulnerability. [Novel412 text mirror](https://www.webnovel.com/it/book/35042645608449005/94695602151027183) discusses lightning damage exceeding both magic and lightning resistance. | Distinct matching elemental category; no cold/fire substitution and no universal automatic paralysis rule. |
| Solar Resistance | [Vampire stat package, Novel43 text mirror](https://www.webnovel.com/id/book/35042645608449005/94086635112683731) contains Solar Resistance−12. Early novel narrative identifies solar spells as a vampire weakness. Sunspot Sphere is described as a solar attribute composed of fire and holy. | Keep solar distinct from fire; no rule for double-applying its component resistances was verified. Point-to-percentage conversion remains the root simulation's explicit estimate. |
| Ground Resistance | [Novel249 text mirror](https://www.ficool.com/story/35042645608449005/94222191394092399) gives Ground Resistance+20 from an Earth Dragon hidden piece. Iron Hill Chaser also names it in [Essence wiki](https://surviving-the-game-as-a-barbarian.fandom.com/wiki/Essence). | Stat existence is verified. Treating this as matching earth/ground elemental damage reduction is a reconstruction inference from its name and Earth Dragon source; no direct damage scene or new knockback/gravity/terrain immunity was established. Category support does not invent attacks or special immunity. |
| Mana Sensitivity | Vampire package above lists+30, but this audit did not verify what the stat does mechanically. Generic elemental-sensitivity explanation is insufficient to equate it with mana regeneration, spell cost, spell damage or magic detection. | Unsupported; no helper grants a guessed gameplay effect. |

Helper contracts: `supports_damage_kind(kind)`, `sensitivity_multiplier(points)`, `cutting_multiplier(points,context)`, `critical(chance_points,damage_multiplier,rng,context)`, `pain_multiplier(points)`, `perceived_pain(intensity,points)`. Context uses `category=weapon`, `weapon_style=slashing`, `damage_type=physical`; `fixed`, `dot`, `secondary` or a nonnegative `fixed_damage` exclude proc calculations. `normal_attack=false` also excludes criticals. Critical result has `critical`, `multiplier`, `chance` (percentage units) and `roll` (percentage units or null). RNG is explicitly passed; endpoint0/100 chances draw no number.
