## Implemented pure helper

`game/stat_mental_rules.gd` exposes `derived_modifiers(values)` and `incoming_duration(status_id,base,values)`. It implements only the sourced Luck/Control/Adaptability directions above, using explicitly estimated coefficients. It does not consume unknown stats, mutate inputs, roll RNG, or access a campaign/save. Positive status applications retain at least one turn; beneficial/unknown statuses and nonpositive base durations are unchanged. Invalid/nonfinite values are ignored. The dedicated headless test passed87 checks with0 failures.

## Integration cautions

- The coefficients in Ch463 combine substats into headline Body/Mind/Special Ability and feed a particular Chaos calculation; they must not silently become generic combat multipliers. Magic Resistance coefficient3 and Possessiveness0 are aggregate examples.
- Negative mental-stat narrative symptoms do not establish a universal tactical penalty. Headaches and altered personalities can have several contributing essence effects.
- English translations vary: Cognition/Perception, Perception/Cognitive Interference, Arbet/Arvet, Erwen/Erwin, Ainar/Aynar. Preserve source labels and avoid double application.
- Primary-novel mirrors were used where Hennovel's early chapters were unavailable; the Ch31 claim is explicitly lower-confidence until a stable chapter text/translation can be checked. No prose from pages labeled fanfiction was used as novel evidence.
