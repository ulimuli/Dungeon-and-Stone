# Physical and resource stat research

This note distinguishes sourced qualitative behavior from numerical reconstruction. `StatPhysiology` is pure. Root integration decides which helpers have live consumers. A helper alone is not a new action, hazard, spirit contract, or complete stat implementation.

| Stat | Evidence and boundary | Helper |
|---|---|---|
| Natural Regeneration | [Chapter 21](https://martialrealm.com/novel/sgb/chapter-19-20-21/) establishes increased natural regeneration and healing of wounds with the Undying imprint. No universal points-to-HP-per-tick formula verified. | Derived natural recovery multiplier, estimated +1% per raw point; zero preserves baseline. |
| Soul Power | [Chapter 22](https://noveltune.net/book/surviving-the-game-as-a-barbarian-2/22.html) identifies the resource for abilities and demonstrates +10 upon leveling. | Additional maximum Soul Power, estimated one resource unit per raw point. |
| Soul Regeneration | [Episode 383](https://hennoveltranslations.org/episodes/stgb-episode-383/) discusses recovery of Soul Power and states MP recovery rate scales with total pool. [Spirit Imprint](https://surviving-the-game-as-a-barbarian.fandom.com/wiki/Bjorn_Yandel/Spirit_Imprint) identifies Soul Regeneration separately in stage six. Exact raw Soul Regeneration conversion is unknown. | Estimated +1% recovery per point. Optional proportional-total-pool model exists separately and must not double-apply the derived multiplier. |
| Weight | [Chapter 31](https://noveltune.net/book/surviving-the-game-as-a-barbarian-2/31.html) explicitly connects this stat to knockback and blunt damage. This is body Weight, not shared-pack inventory weight. | Estimated blunt damage factor `clamp(1 + points × .005, .25, 4)`; nonnegative knockback reduction `1 − 1 / max(1, factor)`, capped below immunity. Caller restricts the damage factor to blunt actions. |
| Hearing | [Episode 625](https://hennoveltranslations.org/episodes/stgb-episode-625/) makes sounds clearer after Hearing +60. [Episode 244](https://hennoveltranslations.org/episodes/stgb-episode-244/) shows locating attacks by sound in smoke. Neither establishes remote sight or silent-trap detection. | Estimated additional audible-contact radius: one tile per 20 points, cap eight. Caller must require an actual sound and enforce propagation. |
| Jumping Power | [Episode 625](https://hennoveltranslations.org/episodes/stgb-episode-625/) names Jump Power. [Episode 228](https://hennoveltranslations.org/episodes/stgb-episode-228/) contrasts Leap distances for different bodies. No general raw-point distance formula verified. | Context helper estimates +1% distance per point on an already available jump. Does not grant Leap, collision bypass, or terrain immunity. |
| Lung Capacity | [Episode 324](https://hennoveltranslations.org/episodes/stgb-episode-324/) explicitly attributes longer breath holding to increased lung capacity. | Context helper estimates +1% breath duration per point. No underwater-breathing or smoke-immunity grant. |
| Natural Energy | [Episode 463](https://hennoveltranslations.org/episodes/stgb-463/) identifies the resource consumed by Spirit Magic. First-spirit acquisition remains unverified. | Resource query requires a separately established contract. No starter Fairy spirit or new acquisition procedure. |
| Bone Density | [Episode 446](https://hennoveltranslations.org/episodes/stgb-episode-446/) shows decreasing Bone Density when Gachabon is erased and increasing height. That establishes observed morphology, not a complete universal conversion. | Research only; no invented general armor/height formula. |
| Height | Episode 244 links increased stature to greater weight in that situation. Raw Height points lack a verified universal conversion. | Research only. |
| Bone Strength | Chapter 31 translates the defensive stat as Skeletal Density, whereas wiki tables also use Bone Strength. Treating these as independently quantified defense stats would risk double application. | Research only pending semantic normalization. |
| Endurance | Named in [Episode 383](https://hennoveltranslations.org/episodes/stgb-episode-383/) and essence tables, but no sufficiently specific raw-stat operational rule was verified. | Research only; no invented HP, food, SP, or AP conversion. |
| Leg Power | Named in Iron Hill Chaser tables in the Essence registry. Distinct movement/carrying/jump effects were not verified. | Research only. |
| Appetite | Chapter 31 names it and describes its benefit as insignificant, without a food-consumption formula. | Research only; do not infer hunger multipliers from its name. |

All coefficients, clamps, grid distances, default durations, and one-point resource conversions in the helpers are numerical gameplay estimates. Their metadata describes helper availability separately from integration status. Signed penalties remain meaningful; malformed/nonfinite query points return neutral values. Neither calculation nor metadata queries alter campaign state or consume randomness.
