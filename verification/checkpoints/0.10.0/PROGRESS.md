# Dungeon & Stone — progress

Updated 2026-11-05, development checkpoint 0.10.0. **Runnable and unfinished. No complete campaign or ending exists yet.** Original-novel fidelity is the target; the entire novel has not been read. All six races remain equal priorities. Logical provisional mechanics for unspecified qualitative rules were also authorized by the user; temporary numerical values are marked unverified in content/tooltips and `REFERENCE_RULES.md`.

## Implemented

- Navigable pixel-art Rafdonia city map with eight clickable/focusable destinations and twelve service figures. Services connect to actual preparation, shopping, repair, storage, recruitment and expedition entry. Twelve unnamed operators represent interfaces; twelve recruitable adventurers have campaign-generated names and appearances. Companion figures are omitted from the city map at the user’s request; generated adventurers remain in the recruitment menu. Placement checks prevent service-sprite overlap and keep the player clear. City geometry, appearance interpretations, recruitment availability and fees are provisional.
- Modular 64px portraits and 32px directional sprites. Ten appearance choices with live preview, Randomize and Apply. Creation customization is free; city changes cost five provisional stones only when something changes. Living companions can also be customized. Appearance persists without affecting combat RNG. Four walking poses, short pixel-aligned movement interpolation, reduced-motion snapping, hit feedback and clipped sprites preserve map readability.
- Six selectable race profiles: Barbarian, Beastman, Draconian, Dwarf, Fairy and Human. Starting attributes are provisional; full racial progression is unfinished. Implemented racial entry mechanics now include Barbarian Undying I, the Beastman Skadia ritual, Dwarf Guardian’s Braces duration, controllable innate Draconian Fear and Human magic assessment/private lessons. These are working entry mechanics, not six complete racial trees. The first Fire lesson is provisional; its exact source acquisition remains unresolved.
- Four areas on two floors: Crystal Cave, Goblin Forest, Land of the Dead and Black Rock Mountain. Eight naturally spawned enemy species, fog/knowledge, traps, procedural connectivity validation, safe arrivals, portal travel and persistent per-area expedition state.
- Melee/ranged/reach combat, AP, accuracy/evasion, armor/resistance, status processing, enemy sight/last sightings, opportunity attacks, manual/aggressive/guard companions, guarded trigger and round resolution, permanent hero/companion death. Root input permits actual dungeon mouse attacks, movement and targeting.
- First-species-only XP; cumulative 6/30/150 progression interpreted from early translated-novel context. Six distinct ordinary kills can reach level 2. The interpretation is qualified in reference notes; later thresholds are not invented. The first increase adds ten Soul Power and one essence slot, without unsourced physical growth.
- Five functional essence components: Blade Wolf anonymous scent contacts without revealing terrain/species/HP, Stone Golem area Suppression that breaks on any positive damage, plus Goblin melee-weapon paralysis/traps; Goblin Archer bow poison/Thief Step; Gnome stationary concealment covering nearby allies. Actual physical drops, adjacent living recipients, confirmation, duplicate rejection, absolute 30-minute deadlines and saved expiry across travel. Ordinary rank-nine drops use the novel's0.01% baseline example. Timing/cost/severity remain provisional.
- Twelve generated recruitable adventurers: two for each of the six races, with melee, reach, shield, bow and mage options. Names and appearances are saved independently of combat RNG. Rejoining retains actual equipment, essences, health, injuries, resources and behavior. Permanent death is tracked by stable IDs. These are generated characters, without the novel’s companion identities or backstories; personal quests/relationship depth are unfinished.
- A human mage casts documented rank-eight Ice Spear through a separate mana pool, single-enemy range/LOS, cooldown and manual/AI control. Wizards cannot absorb essences or gain the ordinary monster-XP SP increase. Their further professional progression is unfinished.
- Mage-assisted essence preservation: a real purchased single-use tube, nearby unexpired physical drop, living unstunned/unparalyzed mage, no combat and enough time to finish by expiry and before portal closure. Confirmation explains consumption and time. Filled tubes survive extraction, storage and saves, can be sold or opened with absorption confirmation, and never refund an empty tube. Duplicate/full-slot/mage/dead recipients are rejected without consuming anything. Cost, weight and15-minute work duration are explicit estimates on the current compressed economy. The minute clock gives a real thirty-minute loose lifetime rather than two coarse actions.
- Twenty-four item definitions, all obtainable through starts, shops, ordinary kills or sealing; Stone Golems supply rank-eight mana stones. The two added essence kits are partial reconstructions, not claims that their unknown components are complete. Equipment requirements, carry limits, stacking, search/sorting, consumables, storage, buying/selling, repair and recovery work. Prices, weights, coefficients and supplies remain provisional. Empty/filled tubes have consistent native pixel icons and concise relevant tooltips.

- New expeditions generate96×64 maps (previously38×25), varied packed chambers, seeded arrival corners, spanning connections and alternate loops. Minimum corridor width is three tiles; forest paths are five. Safe portal chambers, separated encounters and rock cover remain connected. A3×3 footprint can traverse arrival→exit. Zoom, fit and drag-pan controls support the larger map. Old saved expeditions retain their38×25 geography until returning.
- Minute-resolution expedition state, estimated one-/two-minute actions, eight-hour camps, chronological food/recovery/essence/portal deadlines and closure-capped large advances. Schema2 quarter-hour clocks migrate once to schema3 minutes without changing round durations, saved geography or RNG. Fed living allies recover naturally outside combat; starvation prevents natural/camp healing.
- Seven-/ten-day closure returns, monthly entry opening, food charged at every crossed day boundary, eight-hour rests, combat potion restriction and city expenses. Faithful taxes/calendar detail remain unfinished.
- Three visible save slots, atomic writes, checksums, healthy backups, structural/content validation before decoding, backup recovery for checksum-valid malformed campaigns, exact RNG restoration and safe settings normalization. Malformed writes/restores preserve existing data/state.
- Consistent keyboard/mouse/controller end-phase Guard rules, no guarding while incapacitated, stationary enemy Gnome concealment and rejection of zero-health active saves. Noncombat abilities and release actions no longer create negative AP or unsaveable expeditions. Mage UI omits unused SP/level values; unused loyalty is retained internally rather than presented as a completed relationship system.
- Local art/audio, two licensed pixel fonts, title/continue/pause/settings, remapping, text scale, reduced motion, mute/category volume, inspect, combat log and journal. Controller mappings and automated focus tests exist; physical-controller playtesting is still needed.

## Racial and Numbered Item expansion

- `game/numbered_items.json` supplies **three integrated Numbered Items**: No.9425 Ring of the Frost Spirit, No.3112 Guardian’s Braces and No.9712 Club of the Snowfield. The shared catalog/resolvers handle equipment metadata, passives, active effect sequences, targeting, damage, cooldowns, stock, persistence and validation. New entries using supported operations need data and sources; an actually new mechanic needs one reusable resolver. The 1,000 synthetic rows in tests measure validator capacity only and are not playable content.
- The ring seals one chosen essence’s active/passive skills while retaining ownership, attributes and slot occupancy. Initial absorption into an empty ring wearer explicitly warns that the new skill will be sealed. Seals, cooldowns and party builds persist. Removing the ring restores access to skills.
- A living uncontracted Beastman wearing the ring can confirm the Skadia ritual outside combat. It destroys **all worn equipment**, leaves pack/storage untouched and creates the permanent initial Strengthening contract. Actual unequip controls allow safeguarding gear first. Initial cold applies to unarmed attacks only; weapons remain physical. Physical bonuses and the cold damage conversion are numerical approximations. Further awakenings/Beast’s Blood growth remain unfinished.
- Guardian’s Braces prevent5% of damage after ordinary defenses, retaining fractional mitigation in saved item state so small hits receive the proper cumulative benefit. Their active repels enemies and cleanses/protects nearby allies against harmful statuses; it does not prevent direct damage. The Dwarf’s documented1.5× efficiency extends this item’s protection duration. Shared item cooldown/charge state follows transfers, preventing party rotation from bypassing cooldown. Range, round durations, charge conversion/reset and cooldown are estimates.
- Club of the Snowfield adds a separate cold component to a successful normal weapon hit. Physical armor and cold resistance resolve separately; misses, unrelated spells and unequipped weapons do not gain this effect. A lethal extra component grants one death reward, not two. Damage magnitude, ordinary weapon stats and price remain estimates.
- Draconian Fear is innate and can be suppressed/released. Nearby opposing attackers lose10 accuracy within3 tiles and line of sight; those numbers are estimates. It does not stun or skip turns. Dragon Language and its acquisition remain unfinished; true-dragon/boss powers are not granted to starting Draconians.
- Human magic entry includes a permanent deterministic aptitude assessment and paid private Ice Spear instruction. Stable identity/seed determine estimated one-in-five suitability independently of names or combat RNG. Assessment costs10 stones/3 city days; lessons cost600/30 days, with30-day month rollover. Lessons preserve HP, establish the mage profession and mana, and forbid essence absorption. Trained recruits retain their profession when benched/rejoining/loading. These are explicitly estimated fees/times/frequency, not guaranteed Magic Tower admission or every Human career.
- Artifact consignments are limited to one each per campaign. Ring base price1,500 corresponds to a1,500,000 novel market quote; Braces50,000 corresponds to an old≈50million quote. Compression and guaranteed current listings are provisional market implementation, not a faithful economy. Artifact scarcity/price balance still needs natural playtesting and the coordinated economy pass.

## Shared stat foundation

- `game/stat_catalog.gd` is the authoritative registry: **102 registered IDs, all with simulation consumers or informational index calculations**. `STAT_REFERENCE.md` is its generated readable master list, with source evidence, units, bounds and explicit estimates. This is a selected project catalog, not an exhaustive reconstruction of the novel’s thousands of substats. It includes engine outputs and legacy reconstruction attributes as well as named novel stats.
- `game/stat_rules.gd` owns deterministic flat/additive-percentage/multiplicative/minimum stacking, rounding, bounds and source breakdowns. `game/stat_sources.gd` adapts equipment, essences, racial bonuses, saved actor modifiers and temporary statuses, then derives secondary stats. Definitions retain their individual bonus amounts and reference shared IDs. Legacy display fields are compatibility projections rather than a second modifier engine.
- Combat uses resolved attack/armor, accuracy/evasion, damage factors, penetration, raw resistance, range, action budgets/costs, initiative, spell/SP costs/cooldowns, healing and natural recovery. Equipment preview uses the actual calculation without changing inventory, HP, state or RNG. Enemy and opportunity attacks use the same resolved values.
- Goblin Archer's nine documented attribute bonuses are imported exactly. Muscle Mass contributes1% total Strength per point. Known aggregate coefficients are recorded as research metadata, not mistaken for damage reductions. Raw resistance points and legacy percentage reductions remain separate; the raw-point conversion and most derived formulas are marked reconstruction estimates. Unknown essence stat packages remain unknown.
- The in-game STATS panel shows actual values and their sources; item tooltips compare resulting equipment changes. Unspecified qualitative effects now use explicitly labelled provisional interpretations authorized by the user. Saved modifiers use stable IDs; duplicate IDs, malformed values and combined overflow are rejected before restoration, including level-dependent derived values. Fractional natural recovery survives loading without free healing.
